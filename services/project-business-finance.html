The Right Funding Structure for Your Next Business Milestone.

Whether you are setting up a new project, expanding capacity or managing day-to-day working capital, the right funding structure matters. Shreenath Finserve supports businesses in assessing funding needs, preparing financial information and engaging with suitable banks and financial institutions.

What we help with

Practical support, in plain business language.

  • Greenfield and brownfield project finance
  • Term loans for expansion, machinery and infrastructure
  • Working capital limits including cash credit and overdraft
  • Bank guarantees and letters of credit
  • Equipment and machinery finance
  • MSME and business loan advisory
  • Structured debt and lender syndication support
  • CMA data, project reports and financial projections
  • Debt restructuring and refinancing advisory

Who can benefit

Startups with viable projects, MSMEs, manufacturers, infrastructure businesses, renewable energy companies and established corporates.
